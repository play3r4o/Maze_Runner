# M1 — Long crushing junction

M0 is accepted. The developer tested M1's original gates and on 2026-11-11 requested lethal closure, slower movement and longer crossings. This specification replaces the original occupied-gate hold/cancel rule. M1 revision is approved; M2 remains unapproved.

## Observable result

A four-way junction with two arrangements and eight-meter-long stone slabs. Initially NORTH/SOUTH are open and EAST/WEST closed. Press **G** for a two-second warning. Replacement routes open over 1.25 seconds, then old routes close over four seconds. A closing slab kills a player whose body it catches. An outer loop is the longer safe detour. No loot rewards yet: the current trade-off is time versus crossing risk.

M0 remains at `?scene=room`. Mouse/WASD, ordinary wall collision, pause and cursor release remain supported.

## Geometry and authority

- Junction floor 40×40 m; four 12×12 m solid quadrants span coordinates ±2 to ±14. The cross is four meters wide; outer loop stays outside the quadrants.
- Stable gate IDs north/east/south/west. Each closed slab is four meters across, eight meters along the passage, three meters high, extending from six to fourteen meters from the hub. Sideways travel is 4.5 m into solid pockets.
- Full passage outlines, end markings, direction/CRUSH ZONE signs and warning/closing colors show the danger. At 3 m/s the eight-meter crossing takes about 2.7 s, plus clearance for the player's body.
- Pure simulation owns phase/timing/poses. The same rectangles drive visible stone and collision. Fixed five-node topology checks preserve hub/arm return routes; general maze generation is deferred.
- `game/junctionRun.ts` owns the living/crushed state. Swept old-to-new stone bounds test the player's footprint before movement resolution. Only decreasing openness is lethal; the warning zone itself is not a death trigger. Stationary closed gates block normally.

## Timing, death and Retry

1. G validates routes and warns for two seconds. Busy/held-key/cooldown requests do not queue another shift.
2. Replacement pair opens over 1.25 s, then old pair closes over four seconds. Occupancy never pauses or cancels the mechanism.
3. Contact causes one terminal crush result. Movement/shift input stops and cursor releases; gates finish their current motion behind a death screen.
4. Three-second cooldown, then idle. Dead players cannot request another shift.
5. **Retry** resets spawn/look, gate arrangements, pending input and shift count and requests mouse capture from the click. No accounts, health system, inventory loss or persistent progression in this revision.

While alive, Escape/blur/tab hiding pauses as before. Warning and motion timers do not catch up on resume. No player teleport/push to avoid stone; living positions stay outside colliders, and an overlapping swept gate resolves as death.

## Automated checks

- Existing M0 movement/loop/browser regressions.
- Warning, opening-first, separate closing duration, repeated triggers, cooldown and connectivity over repeated changes.
- Eight-meter geometry, physical perimeter return route and crossing duration.
- Physical footprint contact, no premature death during warning/opening, early retreat, late entry, outside-zone safety, static-gate blocking, continued motion after death and clean reset.
- Production browser: blocked/open corridor, crush screen/cursor release, dead input ignored, gate completing closure, Retry controls/reset, escaping during reaction time and pause freezing a warning.
- Clock-controlled scenarios run every animation frame using real input handlers; they do not set game state. M1's longer scenarios have a 90 s wall-time budget for software-rendered CI, with no retries or skipped assertions.

## Human checklist

- [ ] Open the latest deployment: header says M1 / CRUSHING GATES. Read the signs and identify the eight-meter danger passages.
- [ ] Walk NORTH/SOUTH and the longer outer return loop. Closed EAST/WEST block normally.
- [ ] Press G and observe the two-second warning, replacement opening and slower four-second closure. Repeat ten changes.
- [ ] Stand midway down an open passage, request a change, stay still: stone catches you, the screen says Crushed, mouse releases and the mechanism finishes closing.
- [ ] Click Retry: respawn at the hub with initial gates/count and normal mouse/WASD. Repeat death/Retry several times.
- [ ] Try retreating during the warning/early closing. You should survive if your whole body clears the stone's path.
- [ ] Enter late or linger in the long passage: physical stone should kill; being merely near the markings should not.
- [ ] Pause during warning or motion, wait, resume: no timing jump. Holding/repeating G must not queue a shift.
- [ ] Check other directions, corner/edge cases, tab switching, resize and console errors. Report whether four seconds/8 m feel fair; these are tuning defaults.

## Completion and exclusions

Ready for testing after automated checks and visual inspection pass. The revised mechanic requires developer acceptance before M2. No automatic/random shifting, procedural maze, loot, enemies, audio production, imported art, multiplayer or full combat/death economy.
