# M8 — Loot, grid inventory, failure and stash (widened)

Approved 2026-11-12 after M10 + M12 acceptance: “let's postpone the co-op for the moment… Let's create the game first and later deal with the coop. Guess M8 is next with the idea that a lot of item things will be added later as well as weapons, armors, shoes and so on as well as some traders at the base. A bit like Dark and Darker type of inventory and trading to traders.”

Choices made with the developer on 2026-11-12: the maze keeps running while the bag or a container is open (looting is a risk), and stepping back into Sanctuary extracts with whatever is carried.

Co-op (M9, M13) is postponed. This replaces the plan's “six non-stacking slots” with a grid inventory in the style of extraction games: items take rectangles of cells, can be rotated, and are dragged between grids. Weapons, armour, boots and other equipment, plus traders who buy and sell at the base, are **later** milestones. M8 prepares for them (every item has a size, rarity, category and gold value; definitions are data) but adds no equipment slots, gold, trading, durability or crafting. No stacking. Delivered in two stages; stop for human acceptance after stage 2.

Original names and generic treasure only; no names, art or UI copied from any existing game.

## Stage 1 — Items, bag, looting, extraction and stash (implemented)

### Items (`game/items.ts`)

Each definition: `id`, `name`, `size` (width × height in cells), `category` (`valuable`, `material`, `provision`, `relic`), `rarity` (`common`, `uncommon`, `rare`, `epic`, `legendary`, each with a colour), `value` (gold, shown now, used by traders later), `unrest` (T1: provisions 0, materials 1, relics 3, valuables 0) and `noisy` (T3: coins). A non-square item can be rotated a quarter turn. About fifteen items to start; the M7 relic becomes the legendary **Heart of the Maze** (2 × 2, unrest 3).

### Grids (`game/inventory.ts`)

Pure functions on a grid of `width × height` cells holding placed items `{ uid, item, x, y, rotated }`: fits, place, remove, move, first free spot (trying both orientations). Items never overlap or leave the grid. Every item has a unique `uid`; no operation copies one.

| Grid | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Bag (carried) | 6 × 4 |
| Stash (at home) | 12 × 10 |
| Chest | 4 × 3 |
| Monster remains | 3 × 2 |
| Reliquary (the relic's room) | 2 × 2 |
| Dropped sack | 4 × 4 |

### Containers and loot (`game/loot.ts`)

- **Chests** (seeded): inner 2, middle 4, outer 6, in a corner of a room. Never in Sanctuary, the relic room or a trap room; one per cell. Each rolls items from its ring's table: further out means more items and rarer ones.
- **Reliquary**: the relic room holds the Heart of the Maze, under the blue beam as before.
- **Remains**: a husk or skitter killed by any cause leaves remains holding a roll from its ring (skitters drop less). The warden leaves nothing.
- **Sacks**: dropping an item puts it in a sack at the player's feet (joining a sack within 1.5 m if it has room).
- Rolls are seeded per container, so a seed always offers the same chests.

### Looting (the world keeps running)

- **E** within 2 m of a container opens it: the loot screen shows the container's grid and the bag. **Tab** opens the bag alone. The pointer is released to drag items; the maze, traps and monsters keep going and the player stands still. Tab, E or Escape closes the screen and returns to play.
- Drag an item to a free spot to move it; **R** while dragging rotates it. **Right-click** sends an item to the first free spot on the other side. Drag an item out of the bag onto the floor area to drop it into a sack.
- Walking more than 2 m away (being pushed, for example) closes a container. A container keeps what was left in it.
- Hovering shows name, rarity, category, size and value.
- The HUD shows how full the bag is and its total value.

### Extraction, failure and the stash

- Leaving Sanctuary starts the run as before. **Entering Sanctuary again ends it as extracted**, with the bag as it is. The relic is now the most valuable item, not the only goal.
- Death or abandon loses everything in the bag.
- The bag persists between runs (what you extracted is still carried). Between runs, in Sanctuary, **Tab** opens the bag beside the stash; move items freely. Whatever is in the bag when you leave Sanctuary is at risk.
- **Save** (`storage/save.ts`, browser local storage, versioned): bag, stash and a run counter. Leaving Sanctuary saves the bag as empty and marks a run in progress; extraction writes the bag back. Reloading or closing the page mid-run therefore counts as abandoning (no reload to escape death). A missing, corrupt or newer-version save is reported once and replaced by an empty profile; the game always starts.
- The result screen lists what was extracted (or lost) and its value.
- Item ids are unique across runs (they include the run counter), so a retry of the same seed never duplicates an id.

### Development

- F2 map: containers by kind (opened ones dimmed). F2 “Clear save” resets the profile.
- Reports (format 7) carry the bag, every container's contents, the open container and the item counter, so a loot bug replays.

## Stage 2 — The maze reacts to what you carry (plan T1, T3, T5, T6, T8) (implemented)

- **Unrest (T1)** becomes the sum of carried items' unrest: seconds between shifts = max(15, 45 − 6 × unrest). Dropping, jamming or losing an item lowers it at once.
- **Footsteps and noisy items (T3)**: walking makes a noise every 0.5 s that monsters within 6 m hear, plus 2 m per noisy item carried (coins and coin purses), with a clink you can hear.
- **Thread spool (T5)**: a free starter-kit item each run if the bag has none and has room (an extracted spool stays in the bag). With it in the bag, T starts or stops laying a red line on the floor, one point per metre, 150 m per run. Dropping the spool stops it. No rewinding.
- **Gate jam (T6)**: with a gate closing within 3 m, a prompt names the gate and the item. F destroys the item under the pointer in the open bag, or the last item put in the bag with it closed, and holds the closing for 6 s; the slab then finishes closing. Once per gate per shift plan. A held slab blocks like a wall and cannot crush.
- **Cairn and faded marks (T8)**: dying (not abandoning) saves the seed, the death position and that run's chalk (save format 2; the last 20 seeds). A later run of the same seed and generator shows a stone cairn there and the old drawings faded. Lost items are not recoverable.

## Human acceptance checklist (after stage 2)

1. Open chests in each ring and compare what they hold. Drag, rotate and right-click items; drop one and pick it up from the sack.
2. Loot while a monster approaches: the world does not wait.
3. Extract with a full bag; check the result screen, move things to the stash, reload the page, and see that both survived.
4. Die with a full bag: it is gone. Start a run carrying something from the stash and lose it.
5. Close the page mid-run and reopen: the bag is gone.
6. (Stage 2) Feel shifts speed up as the bag fills with heavy items and slow down after dropping them; lay thread and follow it home; jam a closing gate with an item; die and find your cairn on a retry of the same seed.
7. Verdict: does loot make you push or turn back? Bag size, item values, chest counts?

## Automated checks

- Stage 2: unrest per item and the interval it gives; footstep noise only while walking, louder with noisy items; the starter spool added once and only with room; thread one point per metre, stops when out or without the spool; jam range, phase, once per plan, item destroyed, closing held exactly 6 s without advancing the replayed shift, then finishing; traces one per seed, capped, format 1 saves upgraded; reports carry thread and jam. Browser: jam prompt and F with the last item; T lays thread shown on the map; a death leaves a cairn on the next run of that seed only.

- Grid: fits/overlap/bounds, rotation, first-fit, move within and across grids, no duplicated or lost uid after any sequence.
- Loot: seeded chest placement (200 seeds) and exclusions; same contents per seed; outer chests richer than inner on average; remains on monster death, none for the warden.
- Run: E opens only within reach; moves validated against the open container and reach; walking away closes it; dropping makes or joins a sack; entering Sanctuary extracts with the bag; death and abandon empty it; the run continues while a container is open.
- Save: round trip, run-in-progress counts as abandon, corrupt and future formats rejected without breaking start-up.
- Reports round-trip inventory and containers.
- Browser: open a chest, move items by drag and right-click, drop and re-take; extract and see the bag kept after reload; stash moves; death clears the bag.
