# M10 + M12 — Monsters and traps (widened), with the minimal M11 crossbow

Approved 2026-11-12 after M7b: “let's do 10 and 12. For 10 I want more monsters. Some that can be killed by me and others “bosses” that cannot but can be lured into traps. I want … traps. Places you step that trigger traps. Balls rolling, spike from the side/ground, holes opening and anything else.”

This widens the plan's M10 (one stalker) and M12 (one tripwire). Killing monsters needs a weapon, so the plan's minimal M11 crossbow and player health come with it. Delivered in two stages; stop for human acceptance after stage 2. M8, M9 and later remain unapproved.

Traps and monsters are generic adventure and horror tropes built from primitive shapes, with original names. No film names, characters or imagery.

## Stage 1 — Traps and health (implemented)

### Health

The player has 100 health (`PLAYER_HEALTH`), with no regeneration during a run. Damage is logged (`hurt` event), heard (`hurt` sound) and shown as a bar in the side panel. At 0 the run fails once with the cause (`killedBy`); a carried relic is lost. Gate crushes still use `crushedBy`. `isDead(run)` covers both. Bug reports restore a dead player alive with at least 1 health.

### Traps (`game/traps.ts`)

| Kind | Trigger | Effect | Lethal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rolling boulder | Plate in the middle of a straight run of at least 3 static corridors | After 0.6 s a 3.2 m ball rolls at 7 m/s from one end of the run to the other | Yes, to anything it touches |
| Wall spears | Plate mid-corridor | After 0.6 s spears fill the middle 5 m of the corridor for 1.2 s | 40 damage, once |
| Floor spikes | Plate at a room centre | After 0.8 s spikes fill a 3.6 m square for 1.5 s | 45 damage, once |
| Opening pit | Plate at a room centre | After 0.8 s a 3 m square opens for 6 s; a body whose centre is over it falls | Yes |
| Dart volley | Plate mid-corridor | After 0.3 s three volleys at 0, 0.5 and 1.0 s cross the middle 5 m | 12 per volley |
| Swinging blade | None, always moving | Swings 1.6 m either side of the corridor middle every 2.4 s | 35, at most every 1.2 s |

- Plates are 1.2 m squares, slightly darker than the floor, glowing amber while the trap is warning or active. They trigger only when armed, then re-arm after a cooldown (boulder 25 s, spears and spikes 12 s, pit and darts 10 s). Stepping off quickly avoids most damage; standing still on a pit plate means falling.
- **Bodies**: the player and hunting monsters press plates; anything inside a firing trap is hurt (patrolling monsters step around plates and time the blades). That is how the warden is lured to its death.
- **Placement** (`placeTraps`, seeded): inner 2, middle 5, outer 9 attempts. Only lethal kinds (pit, boulder) appear from the middle ring outward. Never in Sanctuary or the cells just outside its entrances, the relic room or landmark rooms, or on gate corridors. One trap per cell. No kind takes more than a third of a ring's traps. Boulder runs use static corridors only, so no gate can cut a roll short.
- Sounds: plate click, boulder rumble for the length of the roll, spike burst, pit grind, dart whooshes, blade swish when it crosses the middle near the player.
- Development switch `traps` in F2 (and `?off=traps`). The debug map shows every trap by kind, outlined amber while firing, with boulder runs dashed.

## Stage 2 — Monsters and crossbow (implemented)

### Monsters (`game/monsters.ts`)

| Kind | Where | Speed (patrol / chase, m/s) | Health | Hit | Dies to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Husk: stooped grey-green figure | 1 inner, 2 middle, 2 outer | 1.1 / 1.9 | 75 (3 bolts) | 20 every 1.2 s | Bolts, any trap, closing gate |
| Skitter: low dark body on thin legs | 2 middle, 3 outer | 1.8 / 3.6 | 25 (1 bolt) | 8 every 0.7 s | Bolts, any trap, closing gate |
| Warden: towering near-black hulk, ember eyes | 1, roams outer and middle | 1.4 / 2.6 | Unkillable by bolts | 45 every 1.5 s | Only a rolling boulder, an opening pit or a closing gate |

The player walks at 3 m/s: husks and the warden can be outrun (the warden only just), skitters cannot. At night every monster is 15 % faster, so the warden then matches the player.

- **Placement** (seeded): never in Sanctuary, the cells just outside its entrances, the relic room or a room with a trap; one monster per cell.
- **Patrol**: walks routes between random cells of its home ring over open corridors only (never through a closed or moving gate) and never into Sanctuary. Patrolling monsters know the floor: they step around plates and time the blades.
- **Sight**: sees the player within 20 m along a clear line (stone, pillars and gates block it). Not while the player is in Sanctuary. Spotting gives a growl (husk, skitter) or roar (warden), then a chase straight at the player.
- **Hearing**: within 22 m, through stone, of crossbow shots, bolt impacts, plate clicks, firing traps and the player being hurt. It walks to the noise, looks around for 3 s, then patrols again. Gates do not draw monsters, so the maze alone never sends them hunting.
- **Search**: losing sight sends it to the last place it saw the player, then back to patrol after 3 s.
- **Hunting** (chasing, investigating, searching) monsters press plates and walk into blades like the player. Any monster inside a trap that is firing is hurt, so leading a hunter over a plate, or into a pit or boulder path the player has set off, kills it.
- **Warden**: bolts stagger it for 1.5 s; spears, spikes, darts and blades for 0.5 s. A boulder, an open pit or a closing gate kills it: “The Warden has fallen. It will return.” It comes back after 45 s in the outer room farthest from the player, with a roar. Its heavy footsteps are heard as it moves.
- A closing gate that slides into any monster kills it.
- Dead husks and skitters stay where they fell.

### Crossbow (minimal M11)

- Left mouse button while the chalk is away fires one bolt along the view direction (level). With the chalk out, the button draws instead.
- 16 bolts per run, no pickups; 0.8 s reload. Bolts fly at 45 m/s for up to 40 m and stop at the first monster or stone. 25 damage.
- The shot is loud: monsters within 22 m come to look.
- A small crossbow is held at the lower right while the chalk is away.

### Development

- `monsters` switch in F2 (and `?off=monsters`): monsters stop and vanish; bolts pass where they were. Freeze also holds monsters (not bolts).
- The F2 map shows monsters as dots (husk green, skitter orange, warden large red, grey when dead), ringed red while chasing.
- Reports (format 6) carry every monster, the monster RNG, bolts in flight, bolts left and reload, and reproduce the same next steps.

## Human acceptance checklist (after stage 2)

1. Find each trap kind, using the F2 map if needed. Trigger it, survive it and die to it once.
2. Learn to spot plates by eye and step around them.
3. Kill a husk (3 bolts) and a skitter (1 bolt). Notice a shot draws others.
4. Lead a hunting husk or skitter over a plate and watch the trap take it.
5. Lure the Warden into a boulder's path, over an open pit or into a closing gate; see the notice, and hear it return about 45 s later.
6. Bring the relic home with monsters on, by day and at night.
7. Verdict: harder in a good way? Which trap or monster should be retuned (counts, speeds, damage, bolts)?
