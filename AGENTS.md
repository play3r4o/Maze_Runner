# Working on Shifting Labyrinth

Read `project_brief.md`, `docs/development_plan.md`, `docs/progress.md`, and the current milestone specification before editing.

- Implement only the currently approved milestone. M0–M7b and M10 + M12 (widened monsters and traps, with the minimal M11 crossbow and player health) are human accepted. M8 (widened: grid inventory, looting, stash) is human accepted. M16 (equipment, rolled gear, melee and shields, gold and traders) and M17 (consumables, spitters, brutes, mimics and the Ram boss) are human accepted. M18 (the Octagon: an eight-sided maze of rooms and doors with sanctuaries on the edge, a lobby gate, a night core extract, a final seal, and rings that seal and turn; see `docs/milestones/M18.md`) is approved. Co-op (M9, M13) is postponed; later milestones require a new instruction.
- Each cloud task already has an isolated checkout. Use it; do not create worktrees unless requested.
- Keep simulation and game rules independent of Three.js, DOM, storage, and networking. Rendering consumes state.
- Prefer small explicit modules, plain types, primitive geometry, and minimal dependencies.
- Preserve user changes. Do not rewrite the brief, change scope silently, or push/publish without authorization.
- Use `npm ci` with the lockfile. Run `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, and `npm run test:browser` (which includes the production build).
- Record test evidence and limitations in `docs/progress.md`. Automated tests do not constitute human acceptance.
- At the milestone boundary, provide launch instructions and a manual checklist, then stop. Do not start the next milestone automatically.

M10 + M12 add player health and pressure-plate traps (stage 1), then monsters, unkillable bosses and the minimal crossbow (stage 2). M8 adds a grid bag, items, containers, extraction with loot, a local stash and save, then item unrest, noisy items, thread, gate jam and cairns. M16 adds equipment, stats, weapons, gold and traders. Still excluded: head/legs/back slots, classes and skills, crafting, durability, quests, stacking, a player map, audio files, wall occlusion, concurrent shift plans, whole-maze movement, input-recording replay, telemetry, status effects, stamina, multiplayer, art assets, gravity, sprint, jumping and map editing. Freeze, teleport and bug reports are developer tools in the F2 panel only. The overhead view is an opt-in developer diagnostic, not a settled player navigation mechanic. Preserve M0 at `?scene=room`, M1 at `?scene=junction` and the accepted M2/M3 maze at `?scene=maze`. The default scene is the M18 Octagon (the M4–M17 square maze stays at `?layout=square`), played as the M7 solo expedition with the M7b pressure rules (unrest, dusk bell, positional sound, free-draw chalk, daily seed), each switchable in F2, plus the M10–12 traps, health, monsters (husks, skitters, the Warden) and crossbow, each danger switchable in F2, plus the M6 debug tools. All tuning numbers live in `src/game/tuning.ts`.

M1 revision authorized 2026-11-11: eight-meter crushing passages, two-second warning, four-second closing. Closing contact pushes the body ahead of the stone with swept solid collision. Crush only when the full body cannot fit against the opposing wall; contact alone is not lethal. Occupancy must not hold/cancel gates. Gates finish after death, and Retry resets the attempt. This supersedes the brief's original early no-crushing rule; see its dated amendment and the updated M1 specification. Do not add the later combat/economy death systems yet.
