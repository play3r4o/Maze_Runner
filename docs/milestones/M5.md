# M5 — Safe shifts in generated mazes

Approved after M4 acceptance (“all works, go next”, 2026-11-11). Stop for human acceptance before M6.

## Result and boundaries

Each generated maze has eight stone gates. Pressing **G** asks for a shift: one closed gate opens, then one open gate closes, with the accepted M1/M2 timing and push-before-crush rules. Every proposed shift is validated before anything moves, and every decision (accepted, rejected or ignored) is logged with its reason. Shifts are still player-triggered; automatic timed shifts are M7.

Reference scenes are unchanged: `?scene=room` (M0), `?scene=junction` (M1), `?scene=maze` (M2/M3).

## Gates (`simulation/mazeGenerator.ts`, version `m5.1`)

1. Gate IDs `g1`..`g8`, chosen after the M4 layout from a separate seeded RNG stream. Odd picks (`g1`, `g3`, `g5`, `g7`) start **open** and are existing corridors; even picks start **closed** and are new corridors between unconnected neighbours.
2. Never inside or touching the Sanctuary plaza, so no entrance is a gate.
3. A gate slab is the corridor's full 8 m × 4 m rectangle. Open, it sits 4.5 m sideways in the solid pillar block beside the corridor: +X for north–south corridors, +Z for east–west ones, or the negative side on the last column/row (`gatePocket`, `gateRect` in `simulation/maze.ts`). No two gates share a pillar block.
4. An open-gate candidate is accepted only if, with it and every earlier gate closed, every cell still reaches Sanctuary and no landmark has a single connection that could cut it off. Result: **static corridors alone connect the whole maze and keep each landmark's second route.** No gate arrangement can isolate a region.
5. If too few such corridors exist, one seeded permanent corridor is added and the search repeats (bounded by the number of unused neighbour pairs).

The hand-built M2 maze's two gates use the same geometry function and are unchanged.

## Shift rules (`simulation/generatedShift.ts`)

- **Plan:** one gate to open (currently fully closed) and one to close (currently fully open). One plan at a time. G during a plan is ignored and logged.
- **Candidate order:** every closed×open pair, shuffled by an RNG seeded from the maze seed and the request number. The same seed and the same sequence of G presses always give the same shifts.
- **Validation** (`validateGatePlan`): known, distinct, correctly posed gates; every cell reaches Sanctuary in the current graph, while both are open, and in the target graph. The first valid candidate is accepted; rejected candidates are counted in the log. If none is valid, the request is rejected, logged and nothing moves. With rule 4 above this should not happen in generated mazes; the path is tested with a small fixture.
- **Phases:** warning 2 s → opening 1.25 s → closing 4 s → cooldown 3 s. Only fully open gates are graph links. The topology revision increases when the set of fully open links changes: normally twice per shift. Validation is repeated at those moments; a failing pose would halt the shift instead of committing it.
- **Physical rules** (`game/generatedRun.ts`): the accepted `resolveMovingWalls` routine. Closing stone pushes the body sideways; death only when the body no longer fits; contact alone is not lethal; occupancy never holds or cancels a gate; walking along the passage while pushed can escape. After death the mechanism finishes; Retry resets player, gates, shift state, log and visits.

## Interface

- HUD panel: ring, shift phase (`Shift in 1.2s`, `G8 opening`, `G7 closing`, `Being pushed · escape`, `Cooling down`), shift count and notice.
- 3D: gate slabs, floor outlines and `G7 / 8m CRUSH` signs at both corridor ends; warning and closing colours as in M2.
- Debug panel (F2): gate slabs and states on the map, topology revision and phase, current plan validation, and a **Shift log** (newest first), e.g. `#1 rev 0 ACCEPTED · G8 opens, G7 closes · Accepted: …`. Each decision is also logged to the browser console with the seed.

## Human acceptance checklist

1. Open the default page: **M5 · SHIFTING MAZE**. Press F2: the map shows eight gates (four open slabs in side pockets, four closed).
2. Press G anywhere. Watch the HUD countdown, the named gate opening, then the other closing. The shift log shows the accepted plan; the topology revision reaches 2 and the count reads 1 shift. Press G again during the shift: it is ignored and logged.
3. Force at least 50 shifts across several seeds (Random). Every room should stay reachable: after each shift, find a way back to Sanctuary without the debug route if you can, and confirm the debug route always exists from where you stand.
4. Stand in the middle of a gate passage that is about to close and do nothing: you are pushed sideways, then crushed. Retry resets everything to the seed's starting state.
5. Repeat near one end of the passage and walk out while being pushed: you escape alive.
6. Visit outer-ring cells while shifts run; check landmark visits and return still work.
7. Reload the same seed and press G the same number of times: the same gates move in the same order.
8. Pause during warning/closing, resume, resize. Spot-check `?scene=maze`, `?scene=junction`, `?scene=room`.

## Automated checks

- `npm ci`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `npm run test:browser`.
- 200 seeds: eight gates, IDs and starting states, outside the plaza, eight distinct pockets, open slabs inside the maze and not overlapping any room, corridor or the plaza; all cells reachable and every landmark free of single-connection cuts **with every gate closed**.
- Plan validation: unknown, identical, wrongly posed and isolating plans rejected with reasons; an all-candidates-isolate request is rejected, logged and changes nothing; busy requests ignored and logged; log bounded to 12; same seed and requests reproduce plans.
- **100 seeds × 100 shifts** (plan requirement): every accepted plan keeps every cell connected at every animation step, runs warning → opening → closing → cooldown → idle, never has the closing gate partly shut while the opening gate is not fully open, ends with exactly the planned swap, adds exactly two revisions and keeps four gates open.
- Three seeds physically: a body in the centre of a closing passage is pushed before it is crushed, the mechanism finishes after death and Retry restores the starting state; a body 1.5 m from the passage end escapes by walking while pushed and is never embedded in stone.
- Production browser: eight gates on the debug map; G runs warning, opening, closing and cooldown with the named gates, revision 2, accepted and ignored log entries and a completed shift; all M0–M4 checks.

## Do not build yet

Automatic or timed shifts (M7), several simultaneous plans, whole-maze movement, run-duration guarantees, teleport, event export or replay tooling (M6), objectives, items, enemies, the M7b pressure rules, art or new dependencies.
