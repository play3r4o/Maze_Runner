# M17 — Survival kit and new threats

Approved 2026-11-12 after M16 delivery. Asked what's next, the developer chose “1 and 2” (confirmed: healing and consumables, then more enemies and bosses). M16 has not been explicitly accepted; the developer moved on.

Original names, primitive shapes and generated sounds only. Delivered in two stages; stop for human acceptance after stage 2. Not included: poison or status effects, stamina, classes and skills, extra slots, crafting, co-op.

## Stage 1 — Consumables (implemented)

| Item | Size | Key | Use | Effect | Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bandage | 1×1 | 1 | 3 s, walking at half speed; any hit cancels it and keeps the bandage | +35 health at the end | 12 |
| Healing Potion | 1×2 | 2 | 1 s to drink (a hit cancels) | +50 health over 5 s, not stopped by hits | 30 |
| Torch | 1×2 | 3 | Instant | Lights the area around you for 120 s; monsters see you from 50 % further while it burns; 3 again puts it out (the torch is spent either way) | 8 |

- Number keys use the first matching item in the bag (rows top to bottom). Only one item at a time. Not in Sanctuary before leaving, not dead.
- Health never goes above the gear maximum.
- The HUD shows a belt: how many of each you carry, and a progress bar while using one.
- Found in chests and remains as common and uncommon rolls (category `consumable`). The Quartermaster sells three bandages, two potions and two torches per refresh (plus thread), and buys consumables.
- Reports carry the item in use, healing over time and the torch.

## Stage 2 — New threats (implemented)

| Monster | Where | Health | Behaviour | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spitter | middle 1, outer 2 | 40 | Keeps 5–10 m away; with a clear line it spits a slow glob (9 m/s, 15 damage) every 2.5 s; backs off when you close in. Globs stop at stone; a raised shield blocks them from the front. | Remains roll |
| Brute | middle 1, outer 1 | 160 | Slow armoured elite; takes 30 % less damage. Before each blow it stops and winds up for 0.8 s (you can step out of reach). Hits 35. | One guaranteed rare or better gear piece plus a roll |
| Mimic | 1 outer chest | 60 | Looks like any chest. Opening it (E) bites for 25, then it chases fast. | The chest's contents |
| The Ram (boss) | one outer room, its lair | 600 | Patrols the outer ring. On seeing you it lowers its head for 1 s, then charges in a straight line at 9 m/s. Hitting you: 50 damage and a shove. Hitting stone or a gate: stunned for 4 s and takes double damage. Weapons and bolts do 20 % damage while it is not stunned. Does not return once killed. | Remains with one epic or legendary gear piece and two valuables |

- New monsters use the same patrol, sight, hearing and Sanctuary rules as M10. The Ram also walks the outer ring when it cannot charge; a shove from its charge pushes you 2.5 m (never through stone). They trigger plates while hunting and are hurt by traps (the Ram dies to a boulder or pit like anything else).
- Sounds: spit, glob splat, brute roar and wind-up grunt, mimic snap, Ram bellow, hoofbeats while charging, impact on stone.
- Debug map shows the new kinds; reports carry globs and new monster states.

## Human acceptance checklist (after stage 2)

1. Use a bandage mid-fight and get it cancelled by a hit; drink a potion and keep fighting; light a torch and notice monsters see you sooner.
2. Buy consumables from the Quartermaster.
3. Fight a spitter (dodge or block globs), a brute (step out of the wind-up), open a mimic.
4. Find the Ram, bait its charge into a wall, punish the stun, take its loot.
5. Verdict: too hard, too easy? Which numbers should change?

## Automated checks

- Stage 1: each consumable's timing, effect, cap, cancel and consumption; keys pick the right item; one at a time; torch duration, light and sight bonus; loot and stock include consumables; reports round-trip.
- Stage 2: placement; spitter range keeping, fire rate, globs blocked by stone and shield; brute damage reduction, wind-up, guaranteed drop; mimic bite and conversion; Ram wind-up, charge line, wall stun, damage rules, death and loot; traps on the new monsters; reports.
