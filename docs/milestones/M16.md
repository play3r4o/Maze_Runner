# M16 — Equipment, stats and traders

Approved 2026-11-12 after M8 acceptance: “it's good. Let's add a character inventory that you can equip stuff and also you can find those in the maze. Like weapon slot, second weapon/shield slot, chest, gloves, amulet, ring, shoes. All those should give you some stats and make you stronger in the maze. Then you should have traders where you can return certain items and sell them for gold and with that gold you can buy other items from them. Once again exactly like Dark and Darker.”

This is a new milestone at the developer's request. It widens the plan's M11 weapon into a gear system and adds the trading the plan's M14 had excluded. It follows the extraction-game conventions the developer pointed to: gear found with random stats by rarity, equipped gear is lost on death, free starter gear, and traders who each buy certain kinds of items and sell their own stock for gold. Original names and primitive shapes only; nothing copied from any existing game. Delivered in two stages; stop for human acceptance after stage 2.

Not included (later, if wanted): head, legs and back slots, a second ring, classes, skills or spells, durability and repair, crafting, quests, player-to-player trade, stacking.

## Stage 1 — Equipment and stats (implemented)

### Slots

Seven slots on a character panel beside the bag: **main hand**, **off hand**, **chest**, **gloves**, **boots**, **amulet**, **ring**. An item fits only its slot type. A two-handed weapon (battle axe, spear, crossbow) leaves the off hand empty and blocks it. The off hand takes a shield or a one-handed weapon.

Drag an item between the bag (or a container, or the stash in Sanctuary) and its slot to equip or take it off; right-clicking gear in the bag equips it (swapping with what is worn). Equipping works anywhere, also mid-run.

### Gear and rolled stats

Every piece of gear is an item **instance**: its kind, a rarity and a list of bonus stats rolled when it is found or stocked.

| Rarity | Bonus stats | Value × |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 0 | 1 |
| Uncommon | 1 | 1.8 |
| Rare | 2 | 3 |
| Epic | 3 | 5 |
| Legendary | 4 | 9 |

Bonus stats are drawn without repeats from: max health, armour, move speed, damage, attack speed, stealth (quieter footsteps), trap resistance. Values scale up with rarity. The base stats of each kind are fixed:

| Slot | Kinds (base stats) |
| --- | --- |
| Main hand | Dagger (fast, 18 dmg, 1.6 m), Shortsword (28 dmg, 2 m), Mace (34 dmg, 1.9 m), Battle Axe (two hands, 48 dmg, 2.2 m, slow), Spear (two hands, 32 dmg, 2.8 m), Crossbow (two hands, bolts 25 dmg, 16 bolts) |
| Off hand | Buckler (armour 10, blocks 50 %), Heater Shield (armour 20, blocks 75 %, slower), or any one-handed weapon |
| Chest | Padded Tunic (armour 15), Chainmail (armour 35, −4 % speed), Plate Cuirass (armour 60, −10 % speed) |
| Gloves | Leather Gloves (armour 5, +5 % attack speed), Gauntlets (armour 14) |
| Boots | Leather Boots (armour 5, +5 % move speed), Heavy Boots (armour 14, −3 % speed) |
| Amulet | Bone Amulet (+10 max health), Silver Pendant (+5 % damage) |
| Ring | Copper Ring (+5 stealth), Signet Ring (+5 trap resistance) |

Rusty Shortsword: free starter weapon with lower damage and no value (traders will not buy it).

### What the stats do

- **Max health**: 100 plus gear. Taking gear off lowers current health only if it is above the new maximum.
- **Armour**: reduces damage from monsters and non-lethal traps by armour ÷ (armour + 100).
- **Move speed**: % change to walking speed.
- **Damage**, **attack speed**: % change to weapon damage and to the time between attacks (bolts too).
- **Stealth**: reduces the footstep-noise radius by that % (max 60 %).
- **Trap resistance**: reduces non-lethal trap damage by that % (max 60 %).

### Weapons

- Left mouse button (chalk away) attacks with the active weapon. A melee swing hits every monster in front (90° arc) within reach and in clear sight, for weapon damage. A melee hit staggers the Warden for 0.8 s (it still cannot be killed by weapons). Swings make a 10 m noise; crossbow shots stay loud (default hearing).
- Crossbow: as before, now an item; 16 bolts per run while carried.
- No weapon: fists, 8 damage, 1.4 m.
- **Q** swaps to the off-hand weapon (if a one-handed weapon is there).
- **Right mouse button** with a shield in the off hand: block. While held, hits from the front 120° lose the shield's block % and walking slows to 60 %.

### Starter gear, death and the save

- Starting a run with an empty main hand equips a free Rusty Shortsword.
- Equipped gear is at risk like the bag: extraction keeps it; death, abandon or leaving the page mid-run loses it. Save format 3 adds the equipment.
- Gear is found in chests and remains (about a third of rolled items; rarer further out). Reports (format 9) carry equipment and gear rolls.

## Stage 2 — Gold and traders (implemented)

- **Gold** is a number on the profile, shown at the base. It is not carried into the maze.
- In Sanctuary before leaving, Tab opens the base screen: tabs for the stash and four traders, with your gold. A trader's tab shows its stock (with prices) beside your stash and bag. Each buys only certain items, for the item's value, and sells a stock at 1.5 × value:
  - **Weaponsmith**: buys weapons and shields; sells weapons and shields.
  - **Armourer**: buys chest pieces, gloves and boots; sells them.
  - **Jeweller**: buys amulets, rings and valuables (coins, gems, chalices, relics); sells amulets and rings.
  - **Quartermaster**: buys materials, provisions and tools; sells thread spools (three per refresh).
- Sell by dragging an item from the stash, bag or equipment onto a trader (or right-click while that trader is open). Buy by dragging from the trader's stock (or right-click); it goes to the stash, and is refused if there is no room or not enough gold.
- Stock: six items per trader (mostly common and uncommon, sometimes rare), seeded, refreshed after every finished run. Bought items leave the stock until the refresh.
- Every sale and purchase is saved at once.

## Human acceptance checklist (after stage 2)

1. Start a run with nothing: you get the Rusty Shortsword. Fight a husk with it.
2. Find gear in chests; read its rolled stats; equip it in the maze; feel armour and speed change.
3. Try a two-handed weapon, a shield block (right mouse) and Q to swap weapons.
4. Extract in gear; die in gear and lose it.
5. Sell loot to each trader; buy a weapon and armour; check prices and that stock refreshes after a run.
6. Verdict: does gear make you stronger in a way you can feel? Prices fair? Which stats or items should change?

## Automated checks

- Gear rolls: seeded; bonus count by rarity; no repeated stat; values in range; value multiplier.
- Slots: only matching items equip; two-handed blocks the off hand; swaps never lose or duplicate items; equipping over a full bag is refused when the old item cannot go back.
- Stats: totals from gear; armour, stealth and trap resistance formulas and caps; speed changes walking; max health changes clamp health.
- Combat: melee arc, reach and sight; attack cooldown with attack speed; Warden only staggered; block reduces frontal hits only; fists without a weapon; crossbow bolts only with a crossbow.
- Profile: starter sword only when the main hand is empty; equipment kept on extraction and lost on death; save upgrade from format 2.
- Traders (stage 2): buy lists; sell for value and buy for 1.5 ×; refused without gold or room; stock seeded and refreshed after a run; gold conserved over any sequence.
- Browser: equip by drag and right-click, stats shown; melee kill; block; buy and sell at the base; persistence after reload.
