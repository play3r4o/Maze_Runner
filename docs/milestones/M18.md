## Rework, 2026-11-13: tall corridors and turning rings

After playing the first version (rooms joined by doorways, with ring doors that only swapped which half was open), the developer said: "mm not sure about this rooms thing. The maze idea is kinda lost here", and wanted the Maze Runner films' direction: "the maze will move on each bell ding. Also let's do the height and scale". They then chose how a turning ring treats you: "you can ride the ring".

The agreed shape, built in three stages:

1. **Ground maze (this stage):** the octagon is filled with narrow corridors and junctions, like the square maze (4 m junctions, 8 m corridors) but with walls 12 m tall. On every dusk bell a ring turns a quarter, and you ride it if you're inside.
2. **Upper level (next, needs a new go-ahead):** wooden bridges on wooden pillars, walkable room roofs and stairs, so you can stay on the ground or go up and look down into the corridors. The core becomes a huge hall with galleries around the bell tower. Adds height and falling.
3. **Hanging blocks (after that):** stone blocks on chains that drop or lift on each bell, sealing and opening corridors and bridges.

The developer's references: the films' maze is three circular sections, and the book's is eight sections around the Glade, with fixed doors and paths that change at night in a repeating pattern. Their concept art adds a giant bell tower that drives the maze, blocks hung on chains, bridges at several heights, and the base as a lived-in town.

### Stage 1 as built

- **Layout (generator `o2`):**
  - The same 13 × 13 octagon (109 junctions) with corridors instead of rooms, and walls and gates 12 m tall.
  - Outer ring (distance 4–5), middle ring (2–3), and the core as one open 3 × 3 chamber.
  - Each ring is its own maze (spanning tree inside the ring plus 12 % loops).
  - **Ring doors:** eight per boundary, one per octagon side, at fixed positions, normally open.
  - **Home base:** a three-room yard outside the south side behind the home gate. The seven other bases are sealed.
  - Eight shift gates as before.
- **Rings turn on each bell:**
  - Bell 1 (2:30) turns the outer ring clockwise, bell 2 (3:15) the middle ring anticlockwise, and bell 3 (4:00, nightfall) both.
  - At each bell the turning ring's doors close over 2 s. They push and crush like any gate; the outer ring's doors include the home gate.
  - The ring is sealed for 4 s and turns a quarter halfway through, then its doors open over 2 s onto different corridors.
  - Turns depend only on the run clock (they follow the dusk rule), so bug reports rebuild them from `runTicks`.
- **Riding the ring:**
  - Everything standing in the turning ring's junctions and corridors turns with it: you (your view turns too), monsters (they re-plan their walk), chests and remains, and the ring's corridors, gates, traps and landmarks.
  - The ring doors, the core, the bases and anything standing in a ring door stay put.
  - Chalk, thread, bolts and globs inside a turning ring are lost: the stone grinds them away.
- **Unchanged from the first version:** the lobby gate and E to start, extracting home (the gate shuts behind you), the night core extract (stand on it 5 s), and the final seal at 5:30.
- **Shortcut:** the quarter turn happens in one step while the ring is sealed, with no visible rotation; the maze is simply rebuilt. A smooth visible turn is art-pass work.

## First version (superseded by the rework)

## Layout (stage 1)

- A 13 × 13 grid holds an octagon of **rooms joined by doorways**: 10 m rooms, 4 m doorways 2 m deep (the square maze keeps 4 m rooms and 8 m corridors). Cells within distance 5 of the centre with the corners cut off: 109 rooms.
- **Rings**:
  - Outer ring (distance 4–5, 60 rooms, where you enter, safest).
  - Middle ring (distance 2–3, 40 rooms).
  - **Core** (the centre 3 × 3, one open chamber, deadliest).

  Each ring is its own maze (a seeded spanning tree inside the ring plus 15 % loops).
- Rings meet only at **ring doors**: one doorway per octagon side on each boundary, so 8 between the outer and middle rings and 8 between the middle ring and the core. Half are open at any time.
- **Eight team bases** sit just outside the eight sides. Solo uses the south base; the other seven show a sealed slab and sign in the wall of the room where their gate will be.
- Danger tiers reuse the square maze's spawn rules (the entry ring is the old "inner" tier and the core the old "outer" tier). The octagon has its own counts:

  | Ring | Traps | Chests | Monsters |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Outer ring | 4 | 4 | 2 husks, 1 skitter |
  | Middle ring | 6 | 6 | 2 husks, 2 skitters, a spitter, a brute |
  | Core | 2 | 4 | a husk, 2 skitters, a spitter |

  The core also holds the relic (the Heart), the Ram, the Warden and the mimic chest.
- Eight shift gates work as before. They are never in the core, at a ring door or beside the hub, and they are chosen so the static rooms connect whichever half of the ring doors is open.
- `?layout=square` keeps the M4–M17 square maze (and every older browser test uses it). A bug report picks the layout it was made on. Generator version `o1`; report format 12.

## Match flow (stage 1)

- **Lobby.** You start in your sanctuary with its gate shut. Tab still opens the stash and traders. **E** starts the match: a 3 s countdown, then the home gate slides open and the run clock starts.
- **Home extract.** Step back into your sanctuary after leaving it. The gate shuts behind you, and New run or Retry goes straight to the next match.
- **Night core extract.** At night (240 s, after the third bell) a disc in the middle of the core lights up. Stand on it for 5 s to extract; stepping off resets it. It is meant for one team at a time when PvP arrives. In solo it is simply the second way out.
- **Final seal.** At 330 s every gate seals for good. Anyone still inside is lost ("The labyrinth sealed with you inside").

## Rings seal and turn (stage 2)

- From 60 s and then every 60 s of the run clock, one boundary turns, alternating the outer ring and the core:
  1. 4 s warning (HUD and warning sound).
  2. 2 s for its open ring doors to slide shut. They push and crush exactly like shift gates.
  3. 4 s sealed while it turns.
  4. 2 s for the other half of its doors to open.

  The ways through have moved round by one side (45°). The HUD names the next turn 15 s ahead.
- Ring doors are a pure function of the run clock, so reports replay them from `runTicks`. Shift plans treat the lobby gate and ring doors as open, so shifts keep working while a ring is sealed.
- This is a door rotation, not yet a turning of the stone: rooms keep their place while the openings move. Physically turning a ring's rooms (with what is in them) is a possible follow-up once the feel is judged.

## Not in M18

PvP, other teams and real lobbies, readable room codes, the hub village, art, and a player map. The square maze stays available but is no longer the default.

## Human acceptance checklist (rework stage 1)

Open the deployed game without `layout=`.

1. You start in a small walled yard with a shut **HOME GATE**. Tab opens the stash and traders. **E** starts a countdown and opens the gate.
2. The maze is narrow corridors between **12 m walls**, with a strip of sky above. It should feel like a maze again.
3. Purple **RING DOOR**s lead from the outer ring into the middle ring and then into the core chamber (F2 shows the map).
4. At **2:30** the bell rings: the outer ring's doors and your home gate close. If you stand inside the outer ring, after a few seconds you are somewhere else a quarter of the way round, facing the same way relative to the maze. The doors then open onto different corridors. Check in F2 that the outer ring's layout has turned.
5. At **3:15** the middle ring turns the other way; at **4:00** both turn. Someone in the core doesn't move.
6. Standing in a ring door as it closes pushes you out or crushes you.
7. Getting home and the night core extract still work. Staying past 5:30 still loses the run.
8. `?layout=square` still plays the M17 square maze.
9. Tell us: does the turning feel right, is 12 m the right height, and should the turn be visible (the ring physically rotating) before we add the upper level?
