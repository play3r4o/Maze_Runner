## Layout (stage 1)

- A 13 × 13 grid holds an octagon of **rooms joined by doorways**: 10 m rooms, 4 m doorways 2 m deep (the square maze keeps 4 m rooms and 8 m corridors). Cells within distance 5 of the centre with the corners cut off: 109 rooms.
- **Rings**:
  - Outer ring (distance 4–5, 60 rooms, where you enter, safest).
  - Middle ring (distance 2–3, 40 rooms).
  - **Core** (the centre 3 × 3, one open chamber, deadliest).

  Each ring is its own maze (a seeded spanning tree inside the ring plus 15 % loops).
- Rings meet only at **ring doors**: one doorway per octagon side on each boundary, so 8 between the outer and middle rings and 8 between the middle ring and the core. Half are open at any time.
- **Eight team bases** sit just outside the eight sides. Solo uses the south base; the other seven show a sealed slab and sign in the wall of the room where their gate will be.
- Danger tiers reuse the square maze's spawn rules (the entry ring is the old "inner" tier and the core the old "outer" tier). The octagon has its own counts:

  | Ring | Traps | Chests | Monsters |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Outer ring | 4 | 4 | 2 husks, 1 skitter |
  | Middle ring | 6 | 6 | 2 husks, 2 skitters, a spitter, a brute |
  | Core | 2 | 4 | a husk, 2 skitters, a spitter |

  The core also holds the relic (the Heart), the Ram, the Warden and the mimic chest.
- Eight shift gates work as before. They are never in the core, at a ring door or beside the hub, and they are chosen so the static rooms connect whichever half of the ring doors is open.
- `?layout=square` keeps the M4–M17 square maze (and every older browser test uses it). A bug report picks the layout it was made on. Generator version `o1`; report format 12.

## Match flow (stage 1)

- **Lobby.** You start in your sanctuary with its gate shut. Tab still opens the stash and traders. **E** starts the match: a 3 s countdown, then the home gate slides open and the run clock starts.
- **Home extract.** Step back into your sanctuary after leaving it. The gate shuts behind you, and New run or Retry goes straight to the next match.
- **Night core extract.** At night (240 s, after the third bell) a disc in the middle of the core lights up. Stand on it for 5 s to extract; stepping off resets it. It is meant for one team at a time when PvP arrives. In solo it is simply the second way out.
- **Final seal.** At 330 s every gate seals for good. Anyone still inside is lost ("The labyrinth sealed with you inside").

## Rings seal and turn (stage 2)

- From 60 s and then every 60 s of the run clock, one boundary turns, alternating the outer ring and the core:
  1. 4 s warning (HUD and warning sound).
  2. 2 s for its open ring doors to slide shut. They push and crush exactly like shift gates.
  3. 4 s sealed while it turns.
  4. 2 s for the other half of its doors to open.

  The ways through have moved round by one side (45°). The HUD names the next turn 15 s ahead.
- Ring doors are a pure function of the run clock, so reports replay them from `runTicks`. Shift plans treat the lobby gate and ring doors as open, so shifts keep working while a ring is sealed.
- This is a door rotation, not yet a turning of the stone: rooms keep their place while the openings move. Physically turning a ring's rooms (with what is in them) is a possible follow-up once the feel is judged.

## Not in M18

PvP, other teams and real lobbies, readable room codes, the hub village, art, and a player map. The square maze stays available but is no longer the default.

## Human acceptance checklist

Open the deployed game without `layout=` (add `?seed=N` to share a seed).

1. The header reads **M18 · THE OCTAGON** and you start in a sanctuary facing a shut **HOME GATE**. Walking into it goes nowhere; Tab opens the stash and traders.
2. **E** starts a short countdown; the gate slides open. Walk in: the ring reads **Outer ring**. Rooms are large, joined by short doorways.
3. Turn around and step back into the sanctuary: **Extracted**, the gate shuts. New run puts you back in the lobby.
4. Find a purple **RING DOOR** into the middle ring and then into the **Core** (F2 map helps). The core is one big chamber with the best loot and the Ram.
5. At about 1:00 the HUD warns that the outer ring will seal and turn. Watch its doors close, stay shut, then open on the other sides. At 2:00 the core does the same. Standing in a closing ring door pushes you.
6. After the third bell (4:00), the green disc in the core lights up. Stand on it 5 s to extract through the core; stepping off resets it.
7. Stay out past 5:30: every gate seals and the run is lost.
8. The seven sealed bases show a slab and sign on the octagon's edge.
9. `?layout=square` still plays the M17 square maze.
10. Tell us what to adjust: room size, ring door count, turn timing, night and seal times, and whether the rings should really turn their rooms.
